# Privacy Policy - Sindhi Dictionary

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Sindhi Dictionary ("the app") is an Android dictionary app. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal information ourselves. The app has no user accounts. However, the app shows ads provided by a third party (see below), and that third party may collect certain information.

## Internet Use

The app uses an internet connection to provide its dictionary features and to display ads.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
