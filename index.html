1. Introduction

Welcome to the Sindhi English Dictionary ("we", "our", or "the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data associated with your use of our application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be accessed, how it is used, and the choices available to you. This policy is designed to comply with Google Play Developer Policies and Google Play Data Safety requirements.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Our application operates primarily as an offline-first educational utility:

  • No Personal Accounts: We do not collect, request, or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, device locations, or biometric details.
  • Local Search History & Bookmarks: Your word search history, favorites, and settings are stored locally on your device in an isolated SQLite database. This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.
  • No Selling of Data: We do not sell or share your personal data with data brokers. Advertising is served by Google AdMob, which may access your advertising ID as described in Section 5.

3. Online Features & Service Disclosures

While 100% of the core dictionary operates offline, the App provides two optional interactive network features:

  • AI Educational Chat Assistant: If you add your own Google Gemini API key in Settings and use the AI Bot to ask linguistic or educational questions, the text prompt you type is transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection to Google Gemini API servers to generate an immediate response. The app does not save or transmit your identity with these queries.
  • Encyclopedic Lookup (Wikipedia): When you ask the AI Bot about a term or topic and you are online, the search term may be sent over secure HTTPS to the Wikipedia (Wikimedia) public API to fetch a short factual summary. No account or personal identity is sent.
Data Safety Declaration: Text entered into the AI Bot (including terms looked up on Wikipedia) is ephemeral and transmitted exclusively for App Functionality over secure HTTPS.

4. Device Permissions & Purpose

The App requests only standard Android permissions strictly justified by its features:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required exclusively for optional online features (AI Chat Bot queries and encyclopedic lookups) and checking educational resources. The offline dictionary works completely without internet.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the app to detect whether network connectivity is available to prevent failed network calls when offline.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used only on Android 13+ if you choose to enable the daily "Word of the Day" notification feature. This permission is requested dynamically only upon user opt-in.
  • android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED & android.permission.VIBRATE: Allows the device to reschedule daily Word-of-the-day alarms after phone reboot.

5. Advertising, AI Chat Bot & Third-Party Services

Advertising (Google AdMob). The app shows advertisements served by Google AdMob, including banner ads and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This is handled by Google under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK or Switzerland), a consent form may be shown before personalized ads are loaded. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

AI Chat Bot (Google Gemini). The AI Chat Bot feature is optional. It works only when you enter your own Google Gemini API key in the app's Settings and have an internet connection. In that case, the text of your questions is sent to Google Gemini to generate answers. Without a key, the chat bot uses only the offline dictionary data on your device. Google handles that data under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

In accordance with Google Play's Data Deletion Policy, users maintain full control over all local data:

  • Instant History Clearing: You can delete all search history at any time from inside the app by opening Settings > Data > Clear Search History.
  • Complete Data Erasure: Because all databases and bookmarks are stored exclusively on your device, uninstalling the app permanently purges all local databases, history, and preferences from your device.

7. Age Guidelines & Target Audience

The Sindhi English Dictionary is an educational utility intended for general audiences aged 13 and older, including high school and university students, educators, and language learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 13.

8. Data Security

We utilize standard industry practices to ensure your data remains secure. Because dictionary searches and saved entries reside strictly in local SQLite sandboxes on your device, your data is shielded by Android's application sandboxing architecture.
